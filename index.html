Why list on TasteBites
⚠️ Please read: TasteBites is a guide, not a guarantee.

Restaurant allergy information is provided by the restaurants and by diner reviews. No shared kitchen can promise zero cross-contact. A "TasteBites Certified" badge means the restaurant's training and procedures were checked, not that every meal is allergen-free. Always tell your server about your allergy, and dine at your own risk.

How it works

1. Tell us your allergies

Pick from the top 9 allergens plus gluten. We remember them so you never have to re-explain.

2. Find restaurants that fit

Filter by cuisine, meal and price. Each restaurant shows what it can, can't, or might accommodate.

3. Eat, then rate it

Rate how well your allergy was handled. Restaurants can't delete reviews, so the ratings stay honest.
