Worried about liability?

Many owners worry that saying "we accommodate allergies" invites lawsuits. TasteBites is built around honest, specific information instead of promises:

  • Disclose cross-contact. Your profile lists the allergens used in your kitchen, so diners can decide for themselves.
  • Show what you can reasonably handle. Mark each allergen as "can accommodate", "ask staff", or "cannot accommodate".
  • Diners dine at their own risk. Every profile carries a clear disclaimer, and diners are asked to acknowledge it.
  • Certification verifies training and procedures, not that every meal is 100% safe.

Check with your own lawyer or insurer about your situation.
