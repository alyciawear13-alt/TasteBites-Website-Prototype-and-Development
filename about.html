Our mission

Help people with food allergies eat out safely and confidently, by connecting them with restaurants that are trained, honest about what they can handle, and proud to show it.

The problem

Going out to eat is one of the main ways people get together. For someone with a food allergy or celiac disease it often means reading menus that just say "ask your server," calling ahead, re-explaining to every server, and still not knowing if the kitchen really understands.

At the same time, many restaurants already do careful allergy work behind the scenes, but rely on word of mouth, so diners never find out. Both sides lose.

What we're building

  • For diners (free): save your allergies once, find restaurants that fit, and see which dishes work for you.
  • For restaurants: a profile that shows your allergen practices, kept up to date, plus optional training and certification.
  • For everyone: honest diner ratings of how allergies were actually handled, which restaurants can't remove.

What makes it trustworthy

  • Certification is earned, not bought, and renewed every year.
  • Serious complaints trigger a review, and a restaurant can lose its certification.
  • Certification verifies training and procedures. It never promises a meal is 100% safe.

Who we are

TasteBites is a student venture from BUS 3310 (Introduction to Entrepreneurship) at Cal Poly, San Luis Obispo. One of our founders has celiac disease and knows this problem first-hand.
